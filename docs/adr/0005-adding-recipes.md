# 5. Adding recipes: one inbox, read on the Mac

Date: 2026-11-04 · Status: accepted

The owner has hundreds of saved Instagram reels. Everything lands in one inbox: the share sheet, pasted or dropped
links and screenshots, and Instagram's "Download your information" saved-posts file (every reel/post URL in it is
queued). Reels are read on the Mac (yt-dlp, Whisper, Apple Vision OCR via the /recipe skill), nothing uploaded; the
owner keeps or skips each. Public recipes come only from sources the owner adds, saved one by one with credit and a
link; no bulk copying of a site's catalogue (Fuji X Weekly's author objects to it). Library in iCloud Drive when it's
on, so every device and Claude share it.
